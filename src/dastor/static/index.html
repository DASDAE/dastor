Keys
Wheel zooms both axes, Shift+wheel the x axis, Ctrl+wheel the y axis. Drag pans. Esc: pan mode. 1-4: point, path, polygon, box. d: select area. Enter: finish a shape. Delete: remove the selection (or the vertex under the cursor). Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo, redo. w: whole view. a: auto contrast.
